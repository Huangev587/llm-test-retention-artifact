# Online Resource 2 materials

This supplementary file is provided for peer review and for publication alongside the article upon acceptance. See README.md for the three offline reproduction commands.

The external test suites are identified by their paths in the [original archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21230015). The local study includes generated responses, validation outcomes and mutation reports. The generation archive additionally contains actual prompts and focal Commons Codec excerpts, with the upstream license and notice in generation/. Full production trees, earlier full prompts, model weights and runtime binaries are excluded.

Inside archives/historical_replication.zip, LICENSE_REVIEW.md, provenance.json and notices/ document the historical inputs and their licence records. Release-planning notes in that archive describe the earlier snapshot. Third-party materials retain their own terms.
